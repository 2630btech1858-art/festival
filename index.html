Celebrating India's Festivals

India is a land of many festivals. Here you can learn about some famous festivals celebrated across the country.

Popular Festivals

Diwali

Diwali is the festival of lights. People light diyas, decorate their homes and burst crackers.

Holi

Holi is the festival of colors. People play with colors and water with friends and family.

Durga Puja

Durga Puja is celebrated mainly in West Bengal to worship Goddess Durga.

Onam

Onam is a harvest festival celebrated in Kerala with boat races and flower rangoli.

Eid

Eid is celebrated by Muslims after the holy month of Ramadan with prayers and feasts.

Christmas

Christmas is celebrated on 25th December to mark the birth of Jesus Christ.
